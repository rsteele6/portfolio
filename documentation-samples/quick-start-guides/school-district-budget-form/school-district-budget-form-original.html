How to Submit the Local School District Budget Form (Original)

Note: This is the original guide before I made edits. To see the edited version, click here.

  1. The law requires that school district budgets be balanced. This means that in the General Fund, the Capital Projects Fund, and the Debt Service Fund, the “Total Revenues” must equal the “Total Expenses.”

  2. The law also requires that the columns in the Form labeled “Prior Year” and “Current Year” must be filled in. The Budget column must also be filled in.

  3. On page 1, for the General Fund, if part of the prior year’s General Fund balance needs to be used to balance the budget, place the balancing amount on the line called“Contribution from Fund Balance” in the Revenues section.

  4. On page 1, for the Capital Projects Fund, if some of the budget year’s revenues are meant to increase the Capital Projects Fund balance, place the balancing amount on the line called “Contribution to Fund Balance” in the Expenses section.

  5. Page 2 of the Form must be completely filled out, signed and notarized.

  6. Page 3 of the Form must be completed for the Enterprise Fund. NOTE: Fill out page 3 of the Form if your district has an Enterprise Fund. If not, you may skip page 3.

  7. Page 4 of the Form is to be completed only for Capital Projects Funds or Debt Service Funds. NOTE: If your district does not use these funds, you may skip page 4.

  8. This budget is a public document and must be kept on file by the school district for seven years. It must be available for public review online and in hard copy at school district headquarters.

  9. Finally, a copy of this budget must be filed by mail to the School District Auditor’s Office at 123 Main Street, Anytown, State 00001 within 30 days after your school board formally adopts the budget.
